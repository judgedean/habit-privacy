Privacy Policy for Habit

Last Updated: October 2026

At Habit, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your personal information and health data.

1. Data Storage

Habits, check-ins, moods, and reflections are stored locally on your device. Insights are generated on-device, and nothing is sent to our servers or shared with third parties.

2. Health and Biometric Data

Apple Health access is optional and read-only when enabled. Face ID or Passcode only unlocks the app on your device; biometric data never leaves your device.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us through the app support channels.
